Method — and what it does not claim

How a rank is assigned

  • The depth-chart feed's pos_rank is rank within a slot, and a team can list the same position in several slots (the 2026 Ravens list two QBs in separate slots).
  • So the rank used here is the ordinal within (team, position), ordered by slot then slot-rank: QB1 is the first quarterback listed, QB4 the fourth.
  • The raw slot and slot-rank stay on every published row so the ordering can be audited.
  • Snap share is the mean of the previous four games, joined through the roster's PFR id because the snap-count feed has no GSIS id.

Limits

  • Depth charts are club-supplied and can lag a transaction. A rank is not a promise about who plays.
  • 'Doubtful' is a probability the league does not quantify; 'Out' is final. They are counted separately.
  • Resting-player and personal-matter rows are in the feed and counted; they are disclosed, not filtered.
  • The effect of depth loss on the line has never been measured here (Q-04).
Caveats are part of the dataset, not a footnote.
