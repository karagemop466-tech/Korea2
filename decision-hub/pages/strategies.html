STRATEGY FRAMEWORK

A competition where the gate is the point.

Single bets, parlays and player-prop combos all enter the same ten-gate screen. A strategy earns a rank only after it survives every gate; the leaderboard is the record of what was tried and exactly why it failed.

How luck is removed

At the strategy level

  • Bootstrap interval on ROI, not a point estimate.
  • Permutation null: the same number of bets at the same prices, thousands of times.
  • Benjamini-Hochberg across every strategy tried, so trying more raises the bar.
  • Held-out seasons touched once.

At the market level

  • A season record is mostly luck: Pythagorean R-squared 0.83, luck persistence 0.019.
  • Any 'coin flip' claim must be one observation per game (FL-DH-01).
  • Sample size floor: 39,240 bets to detect 51% at 80% power.
