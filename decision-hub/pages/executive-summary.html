How to read this site

  • Questions — what must be answered before a decision is reasonable. Sorted by how many decisions each one blocks.
  • Suggestions — proposals with impact, cost, risk and the decision they unblock.
  • Decisions — the three choices that gate everything else, with the evidence for each option.
  • Strategies — the ten gates, why nothing passes, and the leaderboard competition design.
  • Injuries & depth — the new tracker, with the method and its limits stated.
  • Audit — independent recomputation of every published number, with the failures named.

Research and decision support only — not betting advice. Nothing here has passed a validation gate. Values that could not be confirmed from an official source are blank and listed on Flagged for review.
