Q-01 · ANSWERABLE

Does anything in this model beat the closing line?

The split is the point: a known is a measured or documented value; an unknown is stated, not guessed.
What we know / what we do notSource
No. The incremental-information coefficient is -0.019 (p = 0.881) after controlling for the close; the model's spread MAE is 10.39 against the market's 10.25 on the same games.nflverse community data (not NFL-published)
Whether the model adds information in any subset (bucket) even if it loses pooled. Not yet tested by bucket.nflverse community data (not NFL-published)

How to answer: Bucket the residual analysis by favourite size, rest, roof and division, with a Benjamini-Hochberg correction across buckets. Committed predictions.csv already carries model_margin, market_margin and result.

Cost: 2 days   Blocks: D-01 D-03

Verification note: decision-hub/scripts/audit_root.py re-derives the pooled numbers from data/derived/games.csv.gz

Q-02 · ANSWERABLE

Is the ATS market exploitable by simple public angles?

The split is the point: a known is a measured or documented value; an unknown is stated, not guessed.
What we know / what we do notSource
Under the published pooled test, 'no', but that test is an arithmetic identity (FL-DH-01). Under the one-sided test, the home cover rate is 48.91% with a 95% CI of 47.73-50.09% on 6,892 games, two-sided p = 0.073. That is inconclusive, not zero.nflverse community data (not NFL-published)
Whether a specific angle is exploitable. 12 classic angles were screened and none survived Benjamini-Hochberg at q = 0.10.nflverse community data (not NFL-published)

How to answer: Keep the one-sided test; add a per-angle table with the one-sided CI and the corrected p, and publish the intervals next to the point estimates.

Cost: 1 day   Blocks: D-01

Verification note: recomputed from committed data in decision-hub/data/root_audit.json

Q-03 · ANSWERABLE

Are same-game parlay legs correlated enough to change the price?

The split is the point: a known is a measured or documented value; an unknown is stated, not guessed.
What we know / what we do notSource
Yes. QB passing yards vs WR1 receiving yards r = +0.562; the four-leg same-team joint probability is 11.1% against 5.7% under independence, so pricing the legs independently understates the joint by a factor of about two.nflverse community data (not NFL-published)
The correlation's uncertainty, and whether the Gaussian copula parameter is stable across seasons. No interval is published.nflverse community data (not NFL-published)

How to answer: Bootstrap the pairwise coefficients by season and by team, then propagate the interval into the SGP price.

Cost: 2 days   Blocks: D-03

Verification note: the joint-vs-product figures are independently reproduced in the project's own verification record (sgp.* checks)
