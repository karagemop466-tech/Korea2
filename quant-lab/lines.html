How the numbers are built

home_points = mu + HFA + OFF[home] + DEF[away]
away_points = mu        + OFF[away] + DEF[home]
originated spread = (home_points - away_points), positive = home favoured
originated total  = (home_points + away_points)
P(home win)       = Phi(spread / sigma)

OFF and DEF are ridge-shrunk (lambda = 6.0), evidence decays with a 90.0-day half-life, and last season carries 0.5 weight. The three hyper-parameters were chosen on 2002–2015 only; 2020–2026 was never used for tuning. Full detail on Method.

A spread of +2.5 on this page always means the home team is a 2.5-point favourite. That convention was verified against the data (see I1) because the mirror's own documentation describes the opposite sign.
