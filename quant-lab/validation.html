Validation gates

The nine gates every strategy must clear, and the matrix showing which ones it clears.

Nine gates, fixed in advance. A strategy is only "skill" if it survives all of them; otherwise it is published as a lead with its failures attached.

Luck removal in detail

expected wins under the market = SUM p_market
luck z  = (wins - SUM p_market) / sqrt(SUM p_market (1 - p_market))
excess ROI = ROI - (-4.55%)     # -110 on both sides, no edge, is the luck-free expectation

Both quantities are reported for every strategy. The point is not that a strategy with a high ROI is lucky — it is that a high ROI with a luck z near zero is evidence of nothing except that the sample is small.
