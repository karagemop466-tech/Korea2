Method

How the lines are built, how the rules are tested, and how luck is taken out of the answer.

The whole project is three ideas: build a line from data only, test rules against prices, and gate the survivors until luck cannot explain them.

1. Line origination

home_points = mu + HFA + OFF[home] + DEF[away]
away_points = mu        + OFF[away] + DEF[home]

Fit: weighted ridge least squares on every completed game before kickoff.
  weight = 2^(-age_days / 90.0)   (previous season x 0.5)
  penalty = 6.0 on OFF and DEF, none on mu or HFA
Outputs: spread = home - away, total = home + away, P(home) = Phi(spread / sigma)
sigma = 13.671 points, estimated from the model's own out-of-sample residuals.

Hyper-parameters were selected on 2002–2015 by spread+total MAE (36 combinations), then frozen. 2020–2026 was never used for tuning, so every holdout number is genuinely out of sample.

2. Walk-forward discipline

Predictions for a week are produced by a model fitted only on games with an earlier kickoff date. There is no lookahead anywhere: the ratings used for Week 9 of 2026 know nothing about Week 9 of 2026.

3. Strategy families

  • Singles — model-vs-closing-line edges on spread and total, and modelled EV on moneylines.
  • Situational singles — short weeks, rest differentials, byes, wind, temperature, dome, divisional games.
  • Parlays — 2- and 3-leg combinations drawn from the edge pool across different games, one per week.
  • Same-game parlay — spread + total in one game, priced with the measured correlation rather than multiplied.
  • Player props — yardage lines from official game logs; the ROI question is flagged, not answered, because no prop price exists in reachable data.
  • Control — back every home team. This is the vig floor a real strategy must beat.

4. Luck removal

luck z     = (wins - SUM p_market) / sqrt(SUM p_market (1 - p_market))
excess ROI = ROI(detail) - (-4.55%)          # -110 both sides, no edge
bootstrap  = 4,000 resamples of settled bets -> 95% interval on ROI
FDR        = Benjamini-Hochberg over all 19 strategies, q = 0.1

The discipline matters more than any single number: with 19 strategies and no correction, roughly one would look significant by chance. The FDR step is what converts "interesting" into "supported".

5. Reproducing it

bash scripts/fetch_data.sh        # pinned raw data (GitHub only)
python3 scripts/build_dataset.py  # parse + normalise
python3 scripts/model.py          # originate lines, walk-forward
python3 scripts/backtest.py       # settle every strategy
python3 scripts/props.py          # prop board + correlations
python3 scripts/validation.py     # gates, FDR, leaderboard
python3 scripts/verify_official.py# 77 checks vs nfl.com
python3 scripts/build_site.py     # regenerate this site

6. Limitations, stated plainly

  • The model is deliberately simple. It is not competitive with a closing line, and the results say so.
  • Closing prices come from one mirror; a stale price would propagate into every ROI.
  • No injury, weather-forecast, or lineup data is used. Rest, roof, surface, temperature and wind are the only situational inputs available in reachable data.
  • Player props are a method demonstration on six players, not a tested market.
  • Nothing here is betting advice.
