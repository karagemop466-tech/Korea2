Attempted but unavailable

  • Any player-prop price (e.g. a sportsbook or odds aggregator) — not reachable. Prop strategies cannot be settled against a real market. They are published with a -110 assumption, blocked by the sample gate, and marked 'needs review'.
  • NFL rulebook (attempted https://www.nfl.com/the-rules/) — 404 on 2026-11-01. No rule text is quoted anywhere in this project. The correlation work is empirical, not rule-derived. link
  • Opening lines for the modern era — present but only 2021 and 1,088 rows, one book. No opening-to-closing (line-movement) strategy was tested; only closing prices are used. link

Environment note

The sandbox that built this can reach only github.com, codeload.github.com, api.github.com, pypi.org, files.pythonhosted.org and registry.npmjs.org directly; nfl.com pages were read through the page-fetch tool. That constraint is why bulk data comes from a GitHub mirror and why the player-statistics releases (which redirect to a blocked host) could not be ingested.

Built by an automated agent session (branch arena/7b140798-korea2) with no manual input. Every published value traces to a source in Sources; anything unverifiable is listed on Flagged for review. Data capture: 2026-11-01T13:37:20Z.
