What the results say

  • Model-vs-market singles lose. Spread, total and moneyline edges all produce negative ROI in both windows. The model's error (10.647 pts) is larger than the closing line's (10.239 pts), so "the model disagrees with the market" is not information.
  • Parlays amplify the same loss. A 3-leg parlay of the same losing legs turns a −13% edge into a −33% ROI.
  • Structural rules are the only place a positive number appears, and none of them survives significance. Wind unders are the clearest lead (positive in both windows, 6/9 gates) but the confidence interval still spans zero.
  • The control is doing its job. Backing every home team returns the vig floor, so any strategy that cannot beat it is just paying the book.
