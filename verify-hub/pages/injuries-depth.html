VH-5 · INJURY & DEPTH-DEFICIENCY TRACKING

What the league publishes.
And what it does not.

The requirement: track injuries and know any deficiencies in defenses (2nd/3rd/4th-string backups replacing starters) and the same on offense. This register separates what verified official data can satisfy today from what must be inferred - and keeps every unverifiable cell blank.

Key gap, verified 2026-11-12: the official weekly injury report is live and complete on status, but the league publishes no depth strings - the official depth-chart path returns 404. Every 'string' cell below is blank by policy.
