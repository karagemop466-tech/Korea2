Availability graph — expected starter vs actual starter

For every position slot of every club, the official starting lineup of week (the player who was expected to start) is set against the official starting lineup of week (the player who actually started). The designation of each player is the official weekly injury report; the roster status is the official club roster page.

The depth tier column is blank unless the club publishes the depth order itself. A tier (1st string, 2nd string, ...) is published only where the club publishes a depth chart on its own official site — see the depth-order source audit. The chart's date and the club's own "unofficial" label travel with the tier. No tier is inferred from a jersey number, an experience count, a salary or the order of a roster listing.
