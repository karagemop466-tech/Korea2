What this hub is

The official availability evidence the rest of this project was missing. Every row below was read from an official NFL source and links to the page it was read from: the official Game Book of each game (starting lineups, substitutions, Did Not Play, Not Active, venue, roof and surface, game-day weather), the official NFL.com team roster page of each club (the official roster status of every player) and the official weekly injury report (designations).

Nothing here is inferred. A depth tier, a snap count, a replacement tier or a game status that no official source publishes is left blank and flagged, never filled in from a roster listing, a jersey number, an experience count or a salary.

Pages

  • Game Books — one row per captured game: official venue, roof and surface, game-day weather and the Game Book link.
  • Official starters — the official starting lineup of every captured game, both clubs, offense and defense.
  • Inactive lists — the official Not Active and Did Not Play lists, and the players who came off the bench.
  • Availability graph — who was expected to start and who actually started, slot by slot, with the official designation of each player.
  • Per-club view — starters out, replacements named, net injury count.
  • Roster status — the official roster status of every player of all 32 clubs, so that "not listed in the weekly report" can never be read as "available".
  • Depth-order source audit — what each club actually publishes as a depth chart, verified club by club.
  • Flagged for review — every irregularity found, with its source.
  • Sources — the source register.
