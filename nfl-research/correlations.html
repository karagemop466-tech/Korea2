How to estimate it

Match the same event, time, player, line and settlement definition; exclude or separately model voids and pushes. For paired binary wins A and B, estimate pA, pB and pAB, then φ = (pAB − pA pB) / √[pA(1−pA)pB(1−pB)]. It is undefined when a marginal is constant. Estimate uncertainty with a game-clustered bootstrap and report sample size, date range and subgroup stability. For two binary legs, pAB can be recovered from φ and the marginals, subject to valid probability bounds. More than two legs require a joint model; pairwise correlations alone are insufficient. Zero correlation does not imply independence.
