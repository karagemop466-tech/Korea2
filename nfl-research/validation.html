02

Leaderboard & rewards design

Freeze submissions and prices before kickoff. Rank only eligible strategies using a pre-registered paired out-of-sample improvement in proper score versus the same market baseline; show confidence intervals and matched sample size. Use net ROI and closing-line comparison as secondary diagnostics, not as proof. Equal event coverage or matched comparisons prevent selection bias; report abstentions and all trials. Allocate any hypothetical rewards only to strategies passing all gates; ties and uncertainty should produce no winner. There is no funded competition or active leaderboard.

Use a game- or week-block bootstrap to reflect clustered outcomes; adjust comparisons across strategy families and preserve a final untouched test period. Selection and repeated testing still make false discoveries possible. No method eliminates luck.
