Why some values are blank

Source limits that cannot be worked around

No official market prices

The NFL's official betting data goes to operators through an exclusive distributor (Genius Sports); NFL.com does not publish historical market open/close lines or player-prop prices. Market columns are therefore BLANK on every row, closing-line value cannot be measured, and no price-based ROI is published. Policy decision RR-A9 is open for review.

No official depth rank or snaps

The weekly injury report prints Player / Position / Injuries / Practice Status / Game Status and nothing else; there is no league-official depth chart (club depth paths return 404) and no snap-count column. Depth ranks and snap shares are BLANK, never proxied from jersey number, salary or experience (rejected requirement RR-H1).
