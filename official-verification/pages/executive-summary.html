Executive summary — official-source verification

This workspace originates its own NFL lines and measures strategy skill, but it does not publish picks. The single most important finding of this verification capture: most of what the site can verify is verified against official NFL pages, and the rest is deliberately left blank because no official source publishes it.

Bottom line

What holds up

  • NE@DET Week 10: Patriots 10, Lions 35, Final — confirmed on the schedule page and the game centre.
  • NE@DET venue: Munich, FC Bayern Munich Stadium — confirmed on the game centre Game Info block.
  • Lions Kerby Joseph, Cade Mays and D.J. Reed are all Out on the official Week 10 injury report (resolves VR-08/VR-12).

  • Schedule announcement dated May 14, 2026; 18 weeks, 272 games; kickoff Sept 9, 2026 Seattle vs New England.

What is deliberately blank

  • Market open and close lines — NFL.com publishes no odds; the official betting feed goes to operators via Genius Sports.
  • Player-prop prices — no historical prop prices in any source held; no prop ROI is published.
  • Official depth-chart rank (1st/2nd/3rd/4th string) — no league-official depth chart exists; club pages are inconsistent or self-labelled unofficial.
  • Snap counts / snap share — not a published column of the weekly report.

Decision implications

No price-based claims can be made yet

Without a permitted market-price source (RR-A9 open), closing-line value cannot be measured, no strategy can be promoted to live, and the demotion gate (RR-B5) has no subject — all 7 candidates stay PAPER. This is a source limit, not a modelling choice.

Depth and injury features remain blocked

No official depth rank, snap count, inactive list or reserve list is captured, so replacement depth, injury ablations and the O vs D deficiency matrix cannot be computed from official evidence (RR-C2, RR-C5, RR-C15, RR-C27). Game Books are the structured source but are not yet ingested per fixture (S13).
