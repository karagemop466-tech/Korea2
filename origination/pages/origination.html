Our own lines

A shrunk attack/defence points model, refit every week on prior games only, produces a spread, a total and a moneyline for every game from 1999 to 2026. It is deliberately market-free: no closing line, total or price enters the model. The market is used afterwards, to keep score.

How it is built. home_points = μ + HFA + attackhome + defenceaway; away_points = μ + attackaway + defencehome. Ridge penalty on the attack/defence coefficients only, sample weights with a 200-day half-life, refit for every week using only games played before that week's first kickoff. Own spread = predicted margin. Own moneyline = Φ(margin / σ) with σ estimated on the training window only.
