Confirmed irregularities in the analysis itself

  • Elo sign error, fixed. The first build credited the home team with Elo points when the away team won. The lookahead check in verify.py caught it (842 of 842 recomputed games disagreed). After the fix the engine's spread error fell from 10.93 to 10.25 points.
  • Player ids coerced to numbers, fixed. A numeric coercion turned GSIS ids into nulls and merged 1,747 players into one. Caught by a duplicate-key check; the player table is now keyed on the upstream id.
  • A degenerate rule, removed. "Fade the team on a short week" looked reasonable but fired on 12 games, because in this data both teams almost always share the short week. It was replaced by a bye-week fade and the reason is recorded in strategies.py.
