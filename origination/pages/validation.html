Validation gates

Ten gates, fixed in code before any strategy was graded, each aimed at a specific way a backtest lies to you. A strategy is only as good as its weakest gate.

GateRequirementWhy it existsSource
G1Lookahead auditEvery declared input is a pre-game column, and no strategy's win rate is statistically impossible given market prices.gates.py
G2Sample size ≥ 300Fewer tickets than this and the estimate is noise; published as needs review instead of ranked.gates.py
G3Out-of-sampleRules and thresholds are declared before evaluation; published numbers are held-out only.gates.py
G4ROI 95% CI lower bound > 0Percentile bootstrap, 10,000 resamples. The lower bound, not the point estimate, decides.gates.py
G5Permutation p < 0.05Null: each ticket wins with the market's de-vigged probability. 20,000 simulations.gates.py
G6Benjamini-Hochberg q < 0.1Correction across all 31 strategies tested, not one at a time.gates.py
G7Deflated Sharpe ≥ 0.95Bailey & López de Prado, with the number of trials as the deflator.gates.py
G8Calibration vs marketOur Brier score must beat the market's on the same tickets. N/A where a strategy has no model probability.gates.py
G9Season stabilityPositive ROI in at least 60% of seasons with ≥ 20 tickets, minimum 3 seasons.gates.py
G10Split-half consistencyPositive in both halves of the evaluation window.gates.py

Luck removal, in plain terms

  • Bootstrap interval (G4). Resample the tickets; if the middle 95% of the resampled ROI includes zero, the result is consistent with no edge.
  • Permutation against the market (G5). Let every ticket win with the probability the closing price implies. If real results are not better than that, the "edge" was the market's own numbers plus luck.
  • Multiple testing (G6). Test 31 rules at 5% and roughly one and a half will look significant by chance. Benjamini-Hochberg prices that in.
  • Deflated Sharpe (G7). The Sharpe you would expect from the best of 31 random strategies becomes the hurdle.
  • Stability (G9) and split-half (G10). An edge that lives in one season is a season, not an edge.
  • Controls. Two no-information rules land on the vig, and one deliberate leak is rejected. The machinery is calibrated.
