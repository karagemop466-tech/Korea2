What clears zero and what does not. Receiving yards improves error but its probability skill is indistinguishable from zero; receptions, passing yards and anytime touchdown clear zero; rushing yards sits on the boundary. That pattern is credible: passing volume is the most forecastable thing in football, and a trailing average already captures most of it.
