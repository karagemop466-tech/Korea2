Official league source

NFL.com is the only league-operated source used. It is used for spot verification of results (three games, all matching) and to confirm the week-8 2026 schedule and one final score. It is not used for bulk ingestion: no bulk endpoint is reachable without a paid feed, and scraping thousands of pages is not a data pipeline.

  • NFL.com — schedules (official)
  • NFL.com — 2026 week 8 (official)
  • NFL.com — PIT at NE, 2015 week 1 (official)
  • NFL.com — GB at CHI, 2019 week 1 (official)
