NFL line origination, strategies and validation

We originate our own spread, total and moneyline for every NFL game, turn the disagreements into strategies, and then try to break those strategies before trusting them: sample-size gates, bootstrap confidence intervals, permutation tests against the market's own de-vigged prices, Benjamini-Hochberg correction across every strategy tested, a deflated Sharpe ratio, and season-by-season stability. 31 strategies were built and 31 were graded on 38,708 held-out tickets. None of them cleared every gate. That is the headline finding, and it is reported as such.

What the work found

1 · Origination
Our line is honest but not sharp
Held-out 2015-2026: our spread misses by 10.15 pts, the close by 9.79. Where we disagree with the close, the disagreement carries essentially no information (slope 0.010, 95% CI -0.125 to 0.150).
2 · Strategies
Vig wins; nothing survives the gates
The control that bets every home team returns -5.4% — almost exactly the bookmaker's margin. Nothing else produced a positive lower confidence bound on ROI.
3 · Luck removal
The best-looking rule is a small sample
The strongest raw result, SIT_WINDY_UNDER, returns +9.2% on 225 tickets with permutation p = 0.0293, but it fails the 300-ticket sample gate, the confidence-interval gate and the multiple-testing gate.
4 · Correlation
Spread and total are near-orthogonal
Cover/over correlation is 0.0159. Player props are where correlation bites: QB yards vs his leading receiver r = 0.674.
