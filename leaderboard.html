Competition rules

  • Entry. A strategy is a fully specified, data-independent rule. Thresholds are fixed before the holdout is looked at; changing a rule creates a new entry and increments the family size used for multiple-testing control.
  • Settlement. One unit per bet, price actually taken, pushes voided. Discovery 2002–2019 and holdout 2020–2026 are reported separately, and only holdout numbers count for ranking.
  • Qualification. All nine gates must pass — sample, holdout ROI, discovery ROI, significance after Benjamini-Hochberg FDR across the whole family, calibration, season-to-season stability, leave-one-season-out robustness, profit concentration, and price sensitivity.
  • Score. score = ROI(holdout) × 100 × (1 + max(0, luck_z)/3) × (0.5 + 0.5·stability) × (0.5 + 0.5·√(n/300)). A strategy with a large but lucky ROI scores below a smaller, well-sampled one.
  • Rewards (virtual, non-monetary). Gold all nine gates · score ≥ 5   Silver all nine gates · score < 5   Development positive holdout ROI but a gate failed   Retired negative holdout ROI. Nothing here is wagering advice and no real money is involved.
  • Demotion. A qualified strategy that turns negative over its next 100 settled bets drops back to Development. Ranks are recomputed by re-running the pipeline, never by hand.
