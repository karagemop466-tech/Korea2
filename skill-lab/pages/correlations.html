Critical Methodological Discovery: Ex-Post WR1 Selection Bias (+0.2393 Inflation)

Audit Proof (COR-07 vs. COR-08): Selecting a team's top wide receiver ex-ante by prior cumulative target share yields r = +0.3779 (copula ρ = +0.4128, joint-hit multiplier 1.245x across 5,578 team-games). Selecting "WR1" ex-post by whoever led the game in receiving yards inflates the apparent correlation to r = +0.6172 (1.405x) — a pure lookahead selection artifact (+0.2393 bias) that causes naive SGP models to severely overestimate true joint hit rates.
