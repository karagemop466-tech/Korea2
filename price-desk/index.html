What this hub is

This hub closes the loop on the nine named requirements: it records the opening and closing numbers for every market we originate (price-snapshot store), publishes a results feed whose verified rows were checked digit-for-digit against official NFL sources (so backtests and ROI can be computed), ranks strategies on signal quality separately from staking luck, defines the demotion gate, and collects the pre-decision questions and the suggestion review queue. Each table row links its source; values that could not be confirmed against an official source are blank and flagged.
