Price-snapshot store

One row per originated market (game x market type x originating engine), with our fair line, the market's opening and closing numbers, and the difference as separate columns. Opening numbers are blank for every row: no opening-line source covers the origination window (PS-F-01). Closing numbers are archived third-party values, labelled archived-unverified. The difference column is computed as our_fair_line minus market_close_line and inherits the weaker status.
