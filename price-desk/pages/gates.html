Validation gates and the demotion gate

Demotion gate - a live strategy goes back to paper - status: PROPOSED (not yet enforced in code; no strategy is live today, so nothing is being demoted). quant-lab/leaderboard.html states a one-line demotion rule ('a qualified strategy that turns negative over its next 100 settled bets drops back to paper'). This specification generalises that rule, adds measurable triggers, an evaluation cadence, hysteresis and re-promotion criteria. Where the two differ, this spec is the proposal and the quant-lab sentence is the earlier sketch.

Principles

  • Demotion is triggered by signal decay or process failure, never by staking outcomes (staking losses alone do not demote if the signal block is healthy - see price-desk leaderboard split).
  • Every trigger has a stated sample-size floor so noise cannot demote a healthy strategy.
  • Demotion stops staking immediately but keeps logging signals, so evidence can accumulate for re-promotion.
  • The gate is symmetric: the same metrics that promote a strategy demote it.

Cadence: Every Monday 12:00 UTC during the season on settled bets as of the preceding Sunday night; triggers DG-T3/T4/T5 evaluate continuously.

On demotion: Strategy state moves LIVE -> PAPER. Stake size becomes 0 immediately (signals keep being produced and logged). | The strategy's page carries a DEMOTED chip with the trigger id and window that fired. | Rewards accrual stops at the demotion timestamp (no retroactive clawback of virtual points already banked).

Re-promotion: A demoted strategy returns to LIVE only after a fresh paper window of 100 settled flat-stake bets in which flat-stake ROI > 0 AND permutation p <= 0.10 AND mean CLV >= 0, with no process violation in the window. At most one re-promotion per strategy per season; a second demotion in the same season is terminal for that season (strategy returns to research status). (PROPOSED)

Open parameters for owner decision

  • Trailing window length (100 bets proposed; 50 and 150 are reasonable alternatives - trade-off table on the suggestions page, S-07).
  • Permutation p threshold (0.10 proposed).
  • Drawdown cap (25% proposed).
  • Whether re-promotion is allowed at all.
