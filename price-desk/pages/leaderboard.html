Leaderboard - signal separated from staking

signal_rank is computed from the signal block only (deflated Sharpe desc, permutation p asc). Staking outcomes can never raise or lower a strategy's rank. A lucky staking run cannot mask a bad signal. No strategy currently passes the validation gates: all entrants are tier NEEDS_REVIEW or REJECTED in the source ledger, reward_points are 0 across the board, and the rewards pool is unfunded (flagged). This board is a research competition, not a funded one.
