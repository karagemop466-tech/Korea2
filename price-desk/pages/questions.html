Q-01Where can opening and closing odds be obtained from an official source?An authoritative, timestamped odds source covering the origination window (2023-2026) for spread, total and moneyline markets.
  • No official body publishes historical betting odds. NFL.com score and game pages carry no odds (checked on 2026-11-14 while verifying results).
  • The repository's only opening-line archive is origination-lab/data/raw/initial_lines.csv (WSGT, 2021 season, spread/total only) - outside our window.
  • Closing numbers exist only as a third-party archive (games.csv.gz, nflverse-derived; closing_lines.csv 2006-2018 with outcomes).
A licensed or official odds feed for opens and closes.
  • Approach a licensed odds provider or book partnership for a research data licence (this is the only route to genuinely official prices).
  • Check whether the Nevada Gaming Control Board or a state regulator publishes any historical line archive (none is known - would need confirmation).
  • Begin recording our own point-in-time snapshots from a licensed feed going forward - the store schema in price-desk already reserves the columns (PS-1 becomes self-sustaining from the day a feed exists).
