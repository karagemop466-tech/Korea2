The headline. At this read the Week 11 page carries a full game-status column for exactly one fixture — the Thursday game (IND at HOU) — and a practice status only for the other twelve. So 15 of 253 rows carry an Out / Doubtful / Questionable designation, and 238 carry none. That is not a data gap in this capture; it is where the league's week currently is. Reading those 238 blank cells as “cleared” would fabricate 238 healthy players. This desk does not do that.

What this desk found

  1. The Week 11 report is now published. On 2026-11-18 the /2026/reg11 URL still served the Week 10 report and the repository recorded the Week 11 report as unpublished (root flag FL-17). It now serves a Week 11 report with its own heading and fixtures. Recorded on the Flagged page with both read times.
  2. The reg10 URL now renders a Week 11 page title. NFL.com and NFL.com both return “Week 11 of the 2026 Season” in their document title, while the reg10 page body still reads “Injuries - WEEK 10” with Week 10 fixtures. This is the same title/body mismatch earlier sessions flagged, still live on 2026-11-19.
  3. 4 of 13 venues disagree with the register already on the site. NFL.com prints Reliant Stadium where requirements-desk/data/schedule_2026_w11.csv records NRG Stadium. All 13 venues were read from their own game centre; see Fixtures & venues.
  4. “Not listed” is not “available”. Lamar Jackson and Malik Willis do not appear in their clubs' Week 11 tables at all. The page prints no reason. Their absence is recorded as absence, beside the official headline that explains it — never as availability. See Not listed ≠ available.
  5. The injury-description column is only populated where a designation exists. Of 253 rows, 15 carry an injury description, and all 15 also carry a game designation. The two travel together; the desk's verifier fails the build if that ever stops being true.
