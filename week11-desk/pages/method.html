Reproduce the register

cd /home/user/Korea2
python3 week11-desk/scripts/build_register.py --write   # CSV + JSON registers and the manifest
python3 week11-desk/scripts/verify.py                   # strict checks; non-zero exit on drift
python3 week11-desk/scripts/build_site.py               # this site

The register is deterministic: the manifest hash is a SHA-256 of the five data files, and verify.py recomputes it. Re-running the builder on this tree reproduces identical files.

Verification rules this build enforces

  1. Every register row carries an official https source URL and its read clock time.
  2. The committed register hashes match the published manifest.
  3. Row counts, per-club counts and the game-status split recompute from the CSV.
  4. Practice and game statuses are a closed vocabulary taken from the page.
  5. Injury text and game designation co-occur on every row.
  6. Every fixture carries a game-centre URL and a non-empty official venue.
  7. Every delta row's two sources are the two official weekly report URLs, and the delta counts sum to the CSV.
  8. No delta row claims a game-status comparison for a club that published no designation, and no row uses the words “cleared” or “healthy”.
  9. The built pages print the computed values, the session owner and the read time.

What this cannot establish

  • Availability. A blank game-status cell is not availability. Nothing here should be used as a “player is playing” flag.
  • Replacement depth. No source used here publishes depth rank, starter status or snap share. Any 2nd/3rd/4th-string claim would be invention.
  • Predictive value. Practice-status transitions are published; whether they predict anything is untested and this desk makes no claim about it. That requires linked outcomes and a pre-registered evaluation, neither of which exists.
  • Historical replay. This is the first dated Week 11 capture. A series begins here; it does not yet exist.
  • Completeness. The page at this read lists 26 of 32 clubs because six are on bye. It does not list players on injured reserve, and it does not say why.
