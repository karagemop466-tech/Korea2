LIMITS

What this does not establish

  • That any strategy is profitable. The checks confirm the arithmetic, not the edge.
  • That a price is obtainable at any size, or that the market can be beaten.
  • Anything about the 2012-2014 player seasons: the committed pruned extract starts in 2015, so the published player-level coefficients are reproduced on a shorter window and their sample size is smaller here.
  • That a page that loaded today will load tomorrow. Source availability is checked as of the date shown.

Research and measurement, not betting advice. Every figure is computed by the code in this repository from the sources listed on the Source register page; nothing is estimated by hand.
