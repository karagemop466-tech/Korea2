PARLAY PRICING

Independent legs
are the wrong price.

A same-game parlay is priced by multiplying its legs. That is only correct if the legs are independent, and they are not: one offence drives several of them at once. This page measures the correlation, measures the joint probability directly, and puts a Gaussian copula on the measured correlation matrix as an independent cross-check. The gap between the two prices is the finding.

Team-games measured5,896

Seasons 2015-2026.

QB ↔ WR1+0.560

The core same-game pair.

2-leg joint vs product0.336

Independent product would price it at 0.243.

Independent price27.7% too low

Multiplying the legs under-prices this 2-leg ticket by that much relative to the measured joint.

What this does and does not say. It says the legs are correlated and that multiplying them is the wrong price. It does not say a correlated parlay is profitable: no historical player-prop price is committed in this repository, so no prop ROI is computed or implied anywhere on this site.

COPULA

Copula cross-check

Using only the measured pairwise correlations, a Gaussian copula estimates the joint at 0.2430 against a measured joint of 0.2083 and an independent product of 0.1138. The copula is not a re-statement of the empirical number: it is computed from the correlation matrix, so agreement between the two is evidence that the correlation is doing the work.
