INJURY & DEPTH

Who is out is official.
Who replaces him is not in the data.

The NFL’s injury report tells you a player’s practice participation and, later in the week, his game status. It does not tell you whether he is the first-string starter, and it never says whether the man behind him is the second, third or fourth string. Those cells are left blank here rather than guessed, and the page names the exact feed that would fill them.

The honest count. 108 players transcribed across 10 teams from the official Week 10 report. Verified “this player is a starter / N-th string” claims: 0 of 108. The block is a missing feed, not a missing method.
