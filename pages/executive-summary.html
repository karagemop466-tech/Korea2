1 · Goal

Originate our own NFL betting lines (bottom-up from raw official data) instead of following market lines; develop strategies that generate useful signals; run them as a leaderboard competition with rewards for top performers; cover single bets, parlays, and player-prop combos; require every strategy to pass validation gates; explicitly determine correlation values; and remove luck by demanding statistical evidence of skill before any strategy is trusted.

2 · What “originating our own line” means (verified definition)

An originator “aims to originate their own lines using raw data and their own models”, comparing their own probability to the sportsbook’s implied probability to find value — Pinnacle: Bottom-Up Betting / Originator ↗. For NFL models the same official guide names the inputs: player stats, team stats, injury reports, weather for outdoor games — all available officially:

  • Player & team stats: nfl.com/stats/player-stats ↗ · nfl.com/stats/team-stats ↗

  • Injury report + policy deadlines: nfl.com/injuries ↗ · injury report policy ↗ · important dates ↗

3 · Market mechanics (verified against official sportsbook help / regulator)

  • Spread / moneyline / total / prop / parlay / SGP / teaser definitions: DraftKings glossary ↗ · parlay rules (all legs must win; push leg removed; correlated picks restricted) ↗ · prop definition ↗ · FanDuel terms (spread usually 11-to-10 = −110) ↗
  • Parlay card wager = 2+ games/contingencies; grading must be confirmable from official league records: Nevada Gaming Control Board Regulation 22 (PDF) ↗ → NFL official stats are the settlement source of truth.
  • Odds → probability: American-odds conversion formulas ↗ and margin = implied-probability sum − 100% ↗. At standard −110 each side implies 110/210 = 52.38% — the break-even win rate.

4 · Removing luck — validation gates G1–G8 (details on Validation Gates)

  • G1 official-data-only; G2 out-of-sample holdout (Kaggle public/private split + anti-leakage: kaggle.com/docs/competitions ↗); G3 binomial z-test of win rate vs price-implied break-even, p<0.05, N>30 (NIST 7.2.4 ↗); G4 minimum N from NIST sample-size formula (NIST 7.2.4.2 ↗); G5 positive average CLV on holdout (Pinnacle CLV ↗); G6 Bonferroni α/g across candidate signals (NIST 7.4.7.3 ↗); G7 Pearson-r correlation screen on parlay legs + fractional-Kelly staking (NIST correlation ↗, Kelly 1956 original paper, Princeton-hosted ↗, Pinnacle Kelly ↗); G8 settlement audited vs official NFL stats (NV Reg 22).
