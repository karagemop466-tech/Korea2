Gaussian-copula joint probability with these correlations: 12.3% → fair odds 8.13 versus 17.58 under independence (+96.9% pricing error).

Placebo check

Shuffling the same legs across unrelated games gives r = -0.030 on n = 7560. That is what independence looks like, and it confirms the measurement method is not inventing signal.

PRACTICE

How this is used, and not used

  • Legs that share a driver (QB volume with WR1 yards, r = 0.562) cannot be multiplied together: the true joint probability is higher than the product.
  • Legs that trade off (rushing against passing, r = -0.127) go the other way, which is where a book's independent pricing would overpay.
  • Nothing here is a strategy until a real price is compared to the correlation-aware price. That comparison is blocked, and flagged.

Research and measurement, not betting advice. Every figure is computed by the code in this repository from the sources listed on the Source register page; nothing is estimated by hand.
