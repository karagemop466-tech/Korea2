REMOVING LUCK

Separating signal from the bounce of a ball.

A record is a mixture of skill and noise. These tests estimate how much of an NFL season is noise, and whether that noise repeats — because a strategy built on noise looks excellent until it does not.

What this means for strategy design

  • Ranking teams on wins imports luck. Deviation from Pythagorean expectation has essentially no predictive carry-over (r = 0.024), so it cannot be a feature.
  • One-score records do carry a small signal (r = 0.082, p = 0.0165), which is worth testing but is far too small to support a betting edge on its own.
  • Any strategy whose profit is concentrated in close games is a lottery ticket until the sample is large. That is why the gates require thousands of settled bets.
