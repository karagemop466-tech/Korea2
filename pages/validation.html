Why most rejections are structural

The sample-size requirement is brutal: detecting a 52.4% win rate (break-even at -110) at 80% power needs roughly 6809 settled bets. Four seasons of a filtered spot produce hundreds, not thousands, so the honest verdict is "unknown", not "profitable".

Multiple testing compounds it: with many candidates tried, the best one will look good by chance. The deflated-Sharpe threshold below is the bar a zero-skill strategy reaches by luck alone.
