How the pieces fit

  • Research produces the numbers: originated lines, player props, correlations, the ten gates, the leaderboard and the market benchmark.
  • Decide turns the numbers into a choice: what is known, what is missing, what is proposed, and what each proposal would unblock.
  • Evidence proves the numbers: the independent recomputation, the method, the source register and the review queue.
  • Workspaces hold the deeper builds - each lab, desk and audit keeps its own data, its own checks and its own flag register, and none of them was edited by the integration session.

Research and measurement, not betting advice. Every figure is computed by the code in this repository from the sources listed on the Source register page; nothing is estimated by hand.
