PARLAY & PROP COMBOS

The two parlay families we can actually price

Correlated same-game legs

Because the legs share a driver, the joint probability differs from the product. A strategy in this family is only valid if it beats the correlation-aware price, not the naive one. See the correlation page for measured coefficients.

Independent cross-game legs

Multiplication is approximately correct, so the only edge available is per-leg forecasting. Given the measured market accuracy, an edge would have to be large to survive the compounded vig.

Verified pricing reference for teasers, taken from a regulator-filed rulebook: DraftKings betting rules lists a 3-team football super teaser buying 10 points at -120 (1.83) and a 4-team monster teaser buying 13 points at -140 (1.71). Fixed-odds parlay ladders differ by operator and were not confirmed in that document, so the ladder above is labelled illustrative rather than sourced.

Settlement rules the tests obey

  • A spread bet pushes when the margin equals the line; a total pushes when the total equals the line. Operator rulebook
  • Pushes refund the stake and leave the win-rate denominator, which is why n differs from bets placed.
  • Scores and statistics are those the NFL credits. NFL stat rules
  • Games are settled on final score including overtime. Rulebook
