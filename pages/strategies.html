STRATEGY LAB

Five strategies, six gates, no survivors.

Each rule was fixed before outcomes were examined and only uses information available before kickoff. Settlement uses the stored closing price.

Read the ROI column carefully. A positive ROI is not a pass. The gates exist precisely because five strategies will always produce one that looks good.
