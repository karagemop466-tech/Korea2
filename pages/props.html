PLAYER PROPS

Originating prop lines from usage, not opinion.

Forecasts are exponential-weighted means of a player's own prior games only, with the line placed at the forecast plus a pre-test median-residual correction. Every line is testable, which is the point.

The limit is prices, not models. No historical player-prop market prices exist in the official feed, so no prop ROI or closing-line comparison is published. Accuracy and calibration are measured; profitability is unverified and flagged.

Calibration drift is visible and is flagged

Passing yards are still biased. The over rate at our originated passing-yards line is 54.5%, not 50%, because the median-residual correction is estimated on pre-2023 games and the league's passing volume has moved since. Receiving yards (52.4%) shows the same drift at a smaller size. This is published rather than tuned away, and is listed on the Flagged for Review page.

Settlement depends on the official stat book

Prop markets settle on how the NFL credits statistics, not on how a feed summarises them. The league's own Guide for Statisticians defines every credit and is the document any prop strategy ultimately has to respect (NFL stat rules). Official data reaches the betting market through the NFL's exclusive distributor (NFL official data partner), which is why a third-party box score can disagree with a settled prop.

Research and measurement, not betting advice. Every figure is computed by the code in this repository from the sources listed on the Source register page; nothing is estimated by hand.
