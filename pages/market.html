BASELINE

How efficient is the close?

Before any strategy can claim skill it has to beat the closing price. This page measures that price instead of assuming it.

Sign convention, verified. In the source file spread_line is positive when the home team is favoured. We confirmed this two ways: 100% of games with a positive line have a negative home moneyline, and mean home margin rises monotonically across line deciles. Full check on the review page.
