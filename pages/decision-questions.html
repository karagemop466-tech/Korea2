This page separates the answers we have from the gaps we don’t, lists the questions that must be resolved before any capital is committed, and gives a concrete way to discover each missing piece. Unverifiable values are left blank and flagged — never inferred.

No capital decision should be made until the “must-answer” list below is resolved.
Every blank cell is a known gap. Each gap has a discovery suggestion and a source to chase. Suggested answers are proposals, not verified facts.
