SUGGESTIONS REGISTER

Twelve paths.
Choose one before anyone builds.

Each row is a decision, not a task. The recommended path is what this session would do, but it is a recommendation only: the status stays pending until a reviewer accepts, parks or rejects it. Effort is S/M/L, payoff is the expected effect on the project’s ability to make a defensible decision, and risk is what goes wrong if the change is made badly.

How to review. Read the problem, open the source, then pick one path. A suggestion whose evidence you cannot open should be sent back, not approved. Anything marked “park” is not abandoned — it is waiting on a named prerequisite.

SG-04Fix the ATS coin-flip statistic before it is cited againdata/derived/luck.json reports n=13,784 ATS bets and a 50.0% cover rate with p=1.0. 13,784 is exactly twice 6,892 because every game contributes a cover on both sides, so the statistic is 50% by construction and the p-value means nothing. The conclusion ('no exploitable tilt') is still supported, but by the home-side rate of 48.91%, not by that number.Replace the pooled per-team-game rate with a one-sided home cover rate and report the exact binomial p.S / MediumLowpendingApproverecomputable from spread_line, home_score and away_score
SG-05Publish the parlay pricing gap as the headline, not correlation coefficientsThe site leads with r = 0.561 between QB and WR1 yards. A reader cannot act on a correlation coefficient; they can act on 'a 2-leg same-team ticket priced independently is short by 38% on its fair price'.Lead with the measured joint-vs-product table, keep the coefficient as supporting detail, and state plainly that a house pricing legs independently takes an extra margin on correlated tickets.S / HighLowpendingApprovemeasured on 5,896 team-games, 2015-2026
SG-06Give the leaderboard a reward rule that cannot be gamed by variance'Rewards for top performing strategies' is currently undefined, and a naive highest-ROI rule rewards the luckiest of many variants. With 23 variants tested here the best zero-skill per-bet Sharpe rises with the number of tries.Score on gates passed first, then on deflated Sharpe, with a minimum of 300 settled bets and a required positive lower bound. Pay nothing until a strategy clears all ten gates, and cap the number of variants a single entrant may submit.S / HighLow - no money is held by this projectpendingApprovedeflated Sharpe ratio: expected maximum Sharpe under the null

SG-08Quantify what a starting QB absence is worth, before adding any other injury signalLosing a starting QB is the one injury the market is known to move on, and this repository has never measured it.
