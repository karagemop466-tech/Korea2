How the ranking is computed

  1. Every registered strategy is settled on the same out-of-sample window.
  2. Each is scored against the ten gates, with the binomial p-value corrected for the number of tests.
  3. Survivors are ranked by gates passed, then by ROI; ties break on sample size.
  4. Only a 10/10 score is eligible for capital. Anything less stays in the research record.

Research and measurement, not betting advice. Every figure is computed by the code in this repository from the sources listed on the Source register page; nothing is estimated by hand.
