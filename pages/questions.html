OPEN QUESTIONS

What must be answered
before a decision is reasonable.

Seventeen questions, each with the information we hold, the information we are missing, and the decision it forces. Nothing is marked answered unless the answer is computed from a committed file, and that file is linked. Where the honest answer is “nobody can answer this yet”, it says so and names the missing input.

Reading the groups. Answerable now means the data is in the repository and the answer is reproduced on this site. One feed away means a published feed exists that would answer it. Blocked on a decision means no amount of data helps until a human chooses. Cannot be answered yet means the inputs do not exist in any feed this project can lawfully obtain today.
