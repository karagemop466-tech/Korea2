SIGNAL LAB

Twenty-three ideas.
Zero survive ten gates.

Every candidate here is a rule fixed in code before it was measured: a ratings model with a walk-forward fit, rest and bye effects, divisional spots, weather totals, a backup-quarterback rule, mean-reversion fades, prior-season ratings and a luck fade. Each is settled at the closing line in the committed extract, each is corrected for the fact that twenty-three things were tried, and each is compared against random portfolios of the same size at the same prices.

Variants measured23

All settled on 2023-2026 regular-season games.

Qualified0

A strategy needs all ten gates. None got past seven.

Best ROI+1.96%

On 66 bets - far too few to distinguish from climate noise.

Break-even at -11052.38%

The rate needed before any edge exists at all.

Why every strategy fails G1. Gate G1 requires strictly pre-kickoff inputs with a recorded capture. The committed feed holds one closing line per game with no capture time, and no lineup snapshot is committed either, so G1 cannot be evidenced for any candidate. That is a data-pipeline problem, and it is the first thing the Suggestions page recommends fixing.

METHOD

Method

  • Walk-forward only. 2023-2026 regular season; no fitted input sees its own season.
  • Closing-line settlement. Closing line and closing price in the nflverse schedules extract; pushes are returned at zero stake and excluded from the win-rate denominator.
  • Multiplicity. Benjamini-Hochberg q=0.10 across every measured variant in this file.
  • Luck null. 4,000 random portfolios of the same size at the same prices; the share that matched or beat each strategy is reported as G8.
  • Controls included. Betting every home side and betting every under are measured on the same footing, so a “signal” that is just the base rate is visible immediately.

Research and measurement, not betting advice. Every figure is computed by the code in this repository from the sources listed on each row; nothing is estimated by hand.
