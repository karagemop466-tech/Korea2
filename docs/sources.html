What 'official' means here

Only S10-S13 are published by the league itself. The nflverse files are the de-facto standard open mirror of NFL data and are the only practical source of per-game closing prices with American odds, but they are a third-party project, not the league. This project labels them as third party rather than pretending otherwise — the distinction is carried in the table above, not buried in a footnote.

Verification performed

  • League-official spot check (2026): the 2026 standings on NFL.com were compared row by row with the nflverse standings file — 24 of 24 published teams matched on W-L-T, points for and points against.
  • Internal reconciliation: points scored and allowed from the game file equal the standings file for 800 of 800 team-seasons, exactly.
  • Cross-extract reconciliation: the closing line from the game file matches the independent closing-line file exactly on 2,095 of 3,096 home sides, within 0.5 pt on 91.3%, within 1 pt on 97.2%, maximum gap 5.5 pts.
  • Sign conventions: verified empirically rather than assumed (see the field dictionary on the Data page).
  • Price coherence: both sides of each recorded two-way market imply a median hold of 2.4%, consistent with a real two-way market rather than mismatched snapshots.

Open verification gaps

Listed in full on the Flagged for review page: undocumented odds provenance (F1), the player-week mirror (F2), the absence of prop prices (F3), the assumed teaser price (F4), the parlay push convention (F5), the single-season opening lines (F6) and the fact that league-official numeric verification is a spot check rather than an automated diff (F7).
