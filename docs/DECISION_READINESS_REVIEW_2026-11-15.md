# NFL decision-readiness review — 2026-11-15

## Executive answer

**No strategy is currently demonstrated as ready for real-money use or funded rewards.** The repository contains several research dashboards and recommendation queues, but its own latest repository audit documents missing official market prices, incomplete/conflicting results registers, unresolved originator and leaderboard rules, and incomplete availability/depth evidence. The committed decision desk also says no strategy is eligible. Do not treat backtest outputs, paper rewards, or an unverified market proxy as a live edge.

This is a **repository-evidence review**, not a fresh validation of every external source or every row in every dashboard. Official URLs below are supplied for review; prior-session source checks are attributed in the linked repository audit and coordination log, not represented as checks performed by this session. Where the evidence disagrees, both accounts remain visible and the answer is **unresolved**. Proposed clearances are engineering recommendations, not NFL rules or observed results.

The project already has dedicated suggestions and question pages (for example [Suggestions](../pages/suggestions.html), [Decision questions](../pages/decision-questions.html), and [Line Desk suggestions](../line-desk/pages/suggestions.html)). This file consolidates the highest-impact decisions and explains what evidence would make each decision reasonable. It does not choose a proposal or change any model, data, or site navigation.

## Recommended order of work

1. **Freeze policy before more backtests:** price-data policy, canonical result keys, source/use policy, canonical originator, and one preregistration/leaderboard contract.
2. **Make the evidence point-in-time:** immutable price snapshots; official outcomes; daily injury report captures; Game Book starters/inactives; roster/IR and transaction events; venue/schedule versions. Every record needs source, capture time, hashes where raw data are retained, parser version, and explicit conflict/missing states.
3. **Only then evaluate signal:** split seasons chronologically; freeze registrations; compare with controls; publish proper scores, uncertainty, multiplicity adjustment, signal/staking split, and the luck-removal panel. Keep parlays and props in their own divisions behind directly validated joint-probability and settlement gates.
4. **Keep uncertain personnel features out until proved:** verify all 32 teams’ depth provenance, preserve shared/empty slots, never infer string rank, measure designation reliability, and run paired ablations instead of fixed penalties.
5. **Review deployment/reward decisions separately:** a live site is not evidence of a live edge. Confirm source terms, durable storage/alerts, legal/jurisdiction requirements and any reward funding before exposing operational claims.

## Explicit non-answers

The evidence available in the reviewed repository does **not** establish an official opening/closing price feed, positive strategy skill, verified player-prop joint probabilities, a universal 32-team official depth register, reliable injury-designation probabilities, a funded reward pool, or legal permission to wager/pay rewards. These are blank or gated, not values to fill by inference. The linked official pages are starting points for review; they do not, by themselves, answer those project-specific questions.
