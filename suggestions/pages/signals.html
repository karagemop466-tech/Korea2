SIGNALS & ORIGINATION

Originate the line. Then stress-test every signal.

Walk-forward spreads, totals and prop quantiles from official play-by-play feed — then 10 gated tests per strategy.

Origination spec

Form: home=mu+off_home-def_away+hfa; away=mu+off_away-def_home; weighted least squares with ridge lambda 4.0 half-life 180 days; HFA shrunk toward 10-season mean with 60 pseudo-obs; hyperparameters chosen on 2021-2022 (validation MAE 10.354), frozen for 2023+ test.

Accuracy: spread MAE 10.39 pts (model) vs 10.25 pts (closing) walk-forward 2023-2026; incremental regression c=-0.019 (p=0.881) — no incremental information.

Model spec from data/derived/model.json; market accuracy from data/derived/market.json; sign convention proven via diag_signs.json (corr 0.425, moneyline cross-check means +5.67 home fav vs -4.66 dog).

Research program

  • Build point-in-time features only with availability flags (SUG-03, SUG-06).
