Would a thousand random portfolios same size at same prices lose to it >95%? Today none do.

How gates keep skill

  • G4 FDR: BH q≤0.10 across strategies tried — lone p≈0.02 fails when 5 angles tested.
  • G8 luck-adjusted: deflated Sharpe p<0.05 calibrated to variants — wind-under +34.88% n=63 → -3.93% after shrinkage.
  • Public/private split: season-to-date ranks public; private window (last 4 weeks never tuned) final.
  • Result: nothing ranked preferred over noise promoted — every entry REJECTED until all 10 gates including executable.
