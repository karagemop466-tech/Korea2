INJURIES & DEPTH TRACKING

Know who is actually on the field — starters, backups, and who replaces whom.

Official NFL injury-report hub and team depth charts already list info that decides deficiencies. No tracker joins them yet. This page publishes design; no injury signal published until inputs are official, point-in-time and tied to depth tier.

Current capability

What exists today

Official weekly injury hub at nfl.com/injuries/ with per-player practice participation and game-status designations; official team roster pages at nfl.com/teams/<team>/roster; official team depth charts such as colts.com/team/depth-chart listing First/Second/Third/Fourth per position.

What is missing

No automated daily ingestion; injury-policy content URL returns empty body (404-like); roster depth-chart order was previously described as unverified; no mapping from Player→Position→DepthRank exists in the repo.

A WR1 playing through a Questionable tag is not the same as a WR1 Out with a 4th-string replacement; without tracking deficiency tier, an offense/defense strength signal conflates starter vs backup output.

Proposed storage schema — no invented tiers

Depth rank is ordinal official depth chart prints. If team groups WR as LWR/SWR/RWR, preserve that — never invent tier beyond roster sentence.

Suggested build schedule

Suggested cron: every Wed/Thu/Fri 16:05 ET and Saturday/Sunday 23:00 ET fetch /injuries/league/<season>/regN and each team's depth chart + roster; commit JSON with fetched_utc and source_url; block any model that reads deficiency before these files are updated.

  • Each fetch commits JSON with fetched_utc and source_url — only rows with both count.
  • Model reading deficiency before files updated is blocked (G1).
  • Only after several seasons can starter-vs-backup deltas be gated via FDR.

Research and measurement, not betting advice. Every figure is computed from sources listed on the Source register; nothing is estimated by hand. Proposal review required before any site change.
