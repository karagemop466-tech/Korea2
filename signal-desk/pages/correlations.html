Correlations from officially sourced numbers

Requirement 7. Official inputs only, n published with every value, blanks where the official join does not exist.

The rule

A correlation is published only if both variables come from an official source for the same units, and only with n and a confidence interval. Where the official overlap is too small or does not exist, the value is blank and the reason is stated.

Why the parlay pair is blank

Blank on purpose

The parlay pricing this project wants depends on how a quarterback's passing yards move with his top receiver's receiving yards in the same game. The official season-leader pages publish the two quantities for different players, so they cannot be paired. The value is published blank and the reason is stated, rather than replaced with the committed third-party estimate — which exists, and is r = 0.5616 at n = 7,560, but is not official.

What n=32 means

Small n, stated plainly

The team-level correlations are computed over 32 team-seasons. That is enough to describe the season and nowhere near enough to claim a stable effect. The interval is reported with every value for exactly this reason.
