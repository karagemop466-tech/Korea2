Demotion gate: sending a live strategy back to paper

Requirement 8. A promotion-only system is a ratchet. This is the exit.

The gap this closes

The ten static gates decide whether a strategy may go live. Nothing decided what happens after it is live. A promotion-only system is a ratchet: one lucky validation keeps a strategy funded forever.

A rule fires on its trigger alone; no judgement call is needed at the moment it matters.

State machine

There is no transition from paper (demoted) straight back to live on the old evidence. A demoted strategy must re-earn its place on data it has not seen.

Deliberate asymmetry

Slow in, fast out

Promotion is slow (ten gates on a large sample). Demotion is fast (a single rule breach). That asymmetry is deliberate.

Current state

Nothing is live

Nothing is live, so no demotion has fired. The gate is published pre-armed so that the first live strategy has a defined exit before it has a defined entry.
