Origination order: side, then total, then moneyline

Requirement 3. The moneyline must be a function of the side, not a third opinion.

The claimed order holds, mechanically

The published order does hold mechanically: the side is modelled first, the total is estimated from its own attack/defence terms, and the moneyline is Phi(side/sigma). What the order does not buy is accuracy - both originated markets are worse than the market closing line on the same games.

The order

The moneyline must be a re-expression of the side, not an independent third model. If it were fitted separately the three markets could contradict each other and a parlay across them would inherit the contradiction.
StepMarketWhy it is in this position
1Side (spread)The attack/defence model estimates a scoring margin. Nothing downstream can contradict it.
2TotalEstimated from the same terms but a separate quantity, so the side and the total cannot be a by-product of one another.
3MoneylineA re-expression of the side through Phi(side / sigma). Not a third independent model.
