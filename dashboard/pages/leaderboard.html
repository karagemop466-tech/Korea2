Leaderboard Competition — rewards for top gate-validated strategies

Public in-season scoreboard for feedback; private held-out final ranking decides rewards. Modeled on Kaggle public/private leaderboards and the NFL Big Data Bowl ($100,000 total prize pool, NFL-judged). Signal and staking are reported separately: rank order comes from the signal group only (gates, adjusted significance, win rate, sample size); ROI is displayed but never affects ordering, so a lucky staking run cannot mask a bad signal. A demotion gate (D1) returns any live strategy to paper when its signal decays.

The competition structure, gate thresholds and reward tiers below are PROPOSED designs, not validated performance claims. No strategy currently qualifies (all fail the executable gate G9), so no reward is payable.
