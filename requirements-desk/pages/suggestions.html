REVIEW QUEUE · NOT IMPLEMENTED

Suggestions for improving the site and the decisions behind it

Nothing on this page has been implemented. Each item states the problem, one proposal, and the paths a reviewer can choose between. Pick a path and it becomes work; leave it and it stays a proposal. Priority: P1 unblocks the most · P2 material · P3 optional.

How to review. For each row: read the problem, then choose one of the listed options or reject the item. The "unblocks" column shows which requirement ids depend on the choice, so the cost of deferring is visible.

S01P1Capture the same official pages on a schedule, outside the agent
Injury & depth, results, schedule		Every availability fact in this project rests on a capture an agent performed by hand. One capture cannot support a surprise term, a weekly diff, a designation-reliability study or a lookahead-safe backtest. The requirement set asks for a Wednesday-to-kickoff series and none exists.Add a scheduled job that captures the official injury report, the weekly schedule page and the standings page at fixed UTC times, writing each capture to durable storage with a UTC timestamp, source URL and content hash. The job, not the agent, owns the cadence.
Option 1. Run it on GitHub Actions at 15:00 UTC Wed/Thu/Fri/Sat plus a pre-kickoff pass, committing only the manifest and hashes to the repo and the payload to durable storage.
Option 2. Run it in a small external scheduler with object storage; keep the repository free of raw payloads entirely.
Option 3. Keep agent-driven capture for now and accept that no surprise or diff feature can be published until this is built.
R29, R32, R33, R34, R43, R50, R51, R65, R74Two consecutive weeks with ≥3 dated captures per week, each with a content hash, produced with no agent in the loop.source
S02P1Settle the price-data policy in writing before another price backtest is published
Price & market data		Closing-line value, market residual and price-based ROI are all uncomputed because no authorised price source has been approved. Several hubs hold third-party price columns that are labelled archived-unverified; publishing ROI from them would present an unverified number as a result.Write a single policy document naming the authorised feed, its permitted use, the exact definition of opening and closing, the retention period, and the rule that absent prices stay blank. Commit it, then capture against it.
Option 1. Adopt an official/league-authorised feed and pay for it; fastest route to a defensible CLV number.
Option 2. Adopt a licensed commercial odds archive with written redistribution terms; cheaper, still defensible if the terms permit storage.
Option 3. Publish no price-derived number at all for one season and evaluate only proper scores on unpriced forecasts; slowest, but zero terms risk.
R01, R02, R03, R04, R05, R06A dated, committed policy plus ≥1,000 game-market rows with both opening and closing prices, source URL and UTC timestamp.source
S03P1Pick one canonical originator and retire the rest as historical
Origination		Two committed originators disagree by 1.5 to 9.0 points on each of the same 15 games. A residual against the market is meaningless while two different numbers are both called 'our line'.Name one model version as the source of truth, reproduce every displayed fair line from it, and label every other line as historical on its page.
Option 1. Keep the newer walk-forward model and relabel the older one historical.
Option 2. Keep the older model and relabel the newer one experimental.
Option 3. Publish both under the 'two totals until one wins' rule until a pre-registered walk-forward comparison produces an interval that excludes zero.
R03, R04, R11, R12One named originator version reproducing ≥20 published markets, with the competitor visibly retired.source
S04P1Resolve the depth source for all 32 clubs, or stop trying to build deficiency features
Injury & depth		The interactive deficiency matrix, the backup-replacement flag and the string-tier feature all need a depth order. No club depth chart has been parsed, the league depth path returns nothing, the Texans publish depth as an image, and no official snap-count source has been confirmed. Every route is currently at 0 of 32 clubs.Evaluate the two routes in the order the brief specifies - club depth pages first, snap counts second - and publish a club-by-club verdict table. Do not build the deficiency feature until one route is verified for all 32 clubs.
Option 1. Verify club depth pages one club at a time and publish a 32-row provenance table; build nothing until it reads 32/32.
Option 2. Confirm an official snap-count source and derive a starter proxy with a published threshold and sensitivity analysis.
Option 3. Declare depth unobtainable for this season, leave the replacement and string-tier columns permanently blank, and drop the deficiency matrix from scope.
R26, R27, R35, R36, R37, R39, R41, R47, R48, R52, R54Either 32/32 club depth pages parsed into a dated register, or an official snap-count source confirmed for 32/32 clubs over ≥1 season.source
S05P2Capture official inactives so designation reliability can be measured
Injury & depth		The brief asks for 'Questionable' to be calibrated empirically rather than assigned a percentage. That needs designations joined to who actually played. No inactives list has been captured, so no play rate per designation can be computed and the label must not enter any model.Capture the official inactives artefact for every game, join it to the injury report by player and week, and publish a play rate with a 95% interval for each designation before the designation is used as a feature.
Option 1. Capture the weekly inactives article NFL.com publishes and transcribe it row by row.
Option 2. Capture Game Book Not-Active sections per game, which also gives starters and Did-Not-Play.
Option 3. Do neither and keep the designation out of every model, recording it as a permanently blocked feature.
R26, R29, R35, R40, R49, R56≥2 seasons and ≥1,000 designation-to-outcome pairs, each designation carrying a play rate and interval.source

Option 1. Integrate now: one nav, one canonical page per subject, sibling hubs archived behind a dated link.
Option 2. Integrate lazily: keep siblings live, but add a vintage and tier banner to every hub and one top-level index.
Option 3. Freeze new hubs and let the existing ones stand, accepting the duplication.
R07, R15, R17, R19, R72, R73One navigation with 0 duplicate entries and every hub carrying a visible data-vintage and verification tier.source
