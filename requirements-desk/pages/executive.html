What would change the answer

Unblocks the most

  1. A scheduled, agent-free capture of the same official pages (clears 8 requirements).
  2. A written price-data policy and an authorised feed (clears 6).
  3. One canonical originator (clears 4).
  4. A depth source verified for all 32 clubs (clears 11).

Does not unblock anything on its own

  • Adding another dashboard. The repository already has ~26 hubs and they disagree.
  • More backtests. Every backtest inherits the missing price and results coverage.
  • Any injury feature built before inactives and snap counts are captured.
  • A reward schedule without funding, eligibility rules and a jurisdiction review.
