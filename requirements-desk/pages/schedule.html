What a fixture-context feature set may legally contain

Published by this page

  • Kickoff date and time (ET) — a real scheduling fact.
  • Home and away designation.
  • Venue string.
  • Neutral-site marker where the league labels one.
  • Bye status, and therefore each club's week number.

Not published by this page — must stay blank

  • Roof open or closed. Needs the official 90-minute designation, which is not captured.
  • Surface. Not on this page.
  • Wind and precipitation. Needs an NWS grid captured at a recorded time.
  • Travel distance and rest differential. Derivable, but only from these published facts — and not yet built.

Research and measurement, not betting advice. Every number on these pages is computed by the code in this repository from the official pages listed in the source register. Values that cannot be confirmed from an official source are left blank and listed on Flagged for review.
