Q01Strategy eligibility
Can any strategy be used for real money or earn a reward today?		No. Every hub reports zero qualified strategies. The Line Desk lists 7 candidates plus 2 controls with none qualified; the Price Desk reports 28 historical entrants all marked NEEDS_REVIEW or REJECTED. No reward is funded.One frozen candidate registry with a single gate version; a funded pool; a jurisdiction review. The hubs do not share a candidate set or a ranking rule, so their counts are not interchangeable.Select one registry and gate version, run every candidate through it on an untouched holdout, and publish the gate-by-gate outcome. Keep all strategies PAPER until then.Any promotion to LIVE, any reward, any ROI claim.source
Q02Price data
What price-data policy governs market, opening, closing and our own prices?		No official opening or closing price archive has been captured. The strict official-only store has 0 rows. The wider register holds 2,364 game-market rows with 2,364 blank opening fields and 1,956 third-party closing values.An authorised source, its terms, the market coverage, timestamps, immutable raw evidence, and a canonical definition of opening versus closing.Approve the policy in writing first, then test capture on every market type for two weeks and measure coverage before publishing any price-derived statistic.Closing-line value, market residual, price-based ROI, the CLV escrow stage of the leaderboard.source
Q03Origination
Which model is the canonical line originator?		Two committed originators disagree by 1.5 to 9.0 points on each of the same 15 Week 9 games. This is a repository-reported discrepancy, not an NFL fact.The authority file, the version, the sign convention and the tie-breaking rule have not been reconciled.Name one source of truth, reproduce every displayed fair line from it, and reconcile a fixed sample of games and markets against the published output.Any residual against the market, any decomposition view, any origination-alpha claim.source
Q04Results corpus
Is the results corpus complete and canonical enough for backtests?		Two registers with different scopes exist. One holds 44 NFL.com finals for Weeks 7 to 9 plus Week 10 Thursday. The other holds a 1,088-game feed with 22 official, 931 archived-unverified and 135 missing rows. This session added Week 10: 14 of 14 games FINAL from the official schedule page.A single canonical game key, full-season coverage across the evaluation window, and source-level verification for every outcome.Reconcile one feed by NFL game id and require complete official coverage for the pre-registered window. Keep third-party rows separately identified and never add the two registers together.Backtests, ROI, the 136-game pre-registered corpus, opponent-aware ratings.source
Q05Availability
How is a starter defined, and who replaces an injured starter?		The official injury report publishes no depth column, no starter field and no replacement name. This session's capture confirms that: 347 rows across 28 clubs, with practice status and game status only. Game Books identify who actually started, not who was expected to or what string they are.An official expected-starter source; replacement identity; string tier; official snap counts. Club depth pages have not been parsed for any of the 32 clubs.Publish a club-by-club provenance table for both permitted routes. Build no deficiency feature until one route reads 32 of 32.The deficiency matrix, the backup-replacement flag, every depth-based feature, the ablation study.source
Q06Designation reliability
What does 'Questionable' actually mean, and may it be a model feature?		The official report publishes designations, not probabilities. This session counted 90 Questionable, 70 Out, 11 Doubtful and 176 blank game-status cells across 347 listed players. No play rate can be computed from that alone.Designations joined to official inactives over several seasons, with sample sizes, intervals, and role and position context.Capture inactives, join to designations, and publish a play rate with a 95% interval per designation. Only then may the label enter a model, and only as a surprise relative to the prior capture, never as a level.Injury as a model feature, any fixed injury penalty, the designation-reliability gate.source
Q07Correlations
Which correlations are legitimate for each intended use?		Four coefficients were computed this session from official 2026 standings at n=32, each with a Fisher-z 95% interval: win pct vs points for +0.657 [+0.400, +0.818]; win pct vs points against -0.670 [-0.826, -0.419]; win pct vs net points +0.875 [+0.758, +0.938]; win pct vs Pythagorean expected wins +0.872 [+0.752, +0.936]. The Line Desk register adds eight standings coefficients at n=32 and two pooled at n=64.Any player-prop, injury or parlay-leg correlation from an official source. Team-standings coefficients must not be transferred to player legs.Publish separate named correlation families, each with source, definition, n and interval. Where no official evidence exists, leave the value blank rather than substituting a third-party estimate.Parlay and same-game-parlay pricing, prop-combo joint probabilities.source
Q08Parlays and combos
When may parlays and prop combos enter the competition?		The joint-evidence gate is in force: nothing is ranked until a directly validated joint probability and the operator's void/push rules are both available. A marginal correlation does not determine a joint distribution.Authorised historical prices, observed joint outcomes, calibration and uncertainty per combo class, and the exact settlement rules for voids, pushes and partial settlement.Pre-register a joint model, score it directly against observed joint outcomes in chronological holdouts, and publish n, calibration, interval and the settlement contract.Any ranked parlay or prop-combo entry, any combo reward.source
Q09Skill versus luck
What proves signal skill rather than favourable staking or luck?		Signal and staking are ranked separately in the Line Desk, so a lucky staking run cannot move the signal rank. The luck panel now has an official single-season basis: luck standard deviation 1.076 wins across 32 clubs, with the Pythagorean expectation explaining a large share of the win-pct variance.A persistence test. One season cannot show whether luck repeats, and no strategy has a prospective sample, so no promotion or demotion gate has ever fired.Rank by pre-registered benchmark-relative proper score on chronological holdouts with game-cluster uncertainty and multiplicity adjustment, include random and favourites-only controls under identical settlement rules, and publish ROI separately.Any promotion to LIVE, any skill claim, any demotion decision.source
Q10Data governance
How are raw data, retention and source terms governed?		The repository publishes both derived analysis and raw row-level datasets. No source-by-source terms register exists. This hub applies a derived-only policy for the data it added.Terms for each source, permitted storage and redistribution, personal-data exposure, immutable external retention, and a deletion and retention policy.Add a terms register before widening ingestion, move raw rows to access-controlled durable storage, and add freshness and schema alerts.Widening ingestion, retaining raw payloads, any durable-snapshot claim.source
Q11Rewards and legality
Who can receive a reward, and under what rules?		Reward tiers appear on project pages. No funded pool, no eligibility rules and no jurisdiction-specific legal review exist anywhere in the repository.Funding source, eligibility, taxes, age and jurisdiction rules, operator permissions and independent administration.Keep rewards explicitly points-only until a dated funding commitment and a jurisdiction review exist, then publish eligibility, disputes, data policy and payment terms.Any funded competition, any payout.source
Q12Site trust
Can a reader tell, for any number on the site, whether it is verified?		This hub enforces one official source link per table row and leaves unconfirmable values blank. The dated repository audit records 1,538 source-domain flags across 69 pages elsewhere, mostly in hubs built on third-party extracts.A single verification tier label applied to every published table across all hubs, and removal or relabelling of rows that carry no official source.Apply a per-row source rule repository-wide, mark each hub's data vintage, and publish everything that fails the rule on the Flagged for Review page rather than deleting it.Treating any site-wide number as verified.source
