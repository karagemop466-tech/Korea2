Why parlays stay behind a joint-evidence gate

Multiplying marginal probabilities assumes the legs are independent. Within a single NFL game they are not — the same game script drives both. A marginal correlation does not determine a joint distribution either. A combo is therefore ranked only when a directly validated joint probability exists and the operator's void and push rules are both on record. Nothing has passed that gate, so no combo is ranked.
