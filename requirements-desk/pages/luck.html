What this does not prove. One season cannot show whether luck persists, and persistence is the part that matters for separating skill from variance. A season-to-season luck correlation needs at least three seasons of official standings. Until then this panel is descriptive, and no strategy should be promoted on the strength of it.

Why the leaderboard does not rank on win rate

A win-rate ranking rewards whichever club or bettor caught the favourable side of a 1.076-win spread. The project therefore ranks by a pre-registered benchmark-relative proper score with a game-cluster lower bound and a multiplicity adjustment, reports staking outcomes in a separate block, and includes random and favourites-only controls under identical settlement rules.

Research and measurement, not betting advice. Every number on these pages is computed by the code in this repository from the official pages listed in the source register. Values that cannot be confirmed from an official source are left blank and listed on Flagged for review.
