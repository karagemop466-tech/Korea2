Correlations & luck

Correlation values that decide whether a parlay is priced fairly. Parlays on independent events are only fair when the legs really are independent. Several pairs are not, and the copula prices show by how much. Luck is removed by regressing team results on prior-season results; most of the win-loss variance is luck.
