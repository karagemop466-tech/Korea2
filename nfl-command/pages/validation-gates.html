Validation gates

Ten gates decide whether a strategy may earn anything. Gold and Silver tiers need all ten gates (Gold also needs a higher skill score). Bronze needs nine gates, including the mandatory G1, G3, G5, G6 and G10. Tier thresholds are on Strategies. Ten pre-registered signals were also tested for false discoveries.

OFFICIAL value shown with an official link. UNVERIFIED-INPUT computed from third-party inputs; method link shown. BLANK not verifiable from an official source; left empty on purpose.
