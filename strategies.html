CORRELATION WORKBENCH

Measure the joint event, not a story

What to calculate

  • Continuous outcomes: Pearson correlation for linear dependence and Spearman rank correlation for monotonic dependence; compute within matched games and report sample size and uncertainty.
  • Binary leg outcomes: estimate each leg's hit probability, joint hit rate and lift over independence; use a binary association measure such as phi as a descriptive supplement.
  • Dependence-aware probability: publish P(A ∩ B) directly or from a validated conditional model. The product P(A) × P(B) is justified only under independence.
  • Stability: cluster/bootstrap by game-week; examine teams, seasons and game scripts separately. Avoid interpreting tiny subgroups as repeatable signal.
