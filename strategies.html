Line construction & expected value

For a binary no-push bet, a fair decimal price is 1 / p. At decimal odds d, expected net return per unit is p × d − 1. With win, loss and push probabilities, EV = p(win) × (d − 1) − p(loss), assuming a push refunds stake. For parlays, price the joint outcome and all settlement states; do not multiply marginal probabilities unless independence is justified. These are mathematical definitions, not verified market prices.
