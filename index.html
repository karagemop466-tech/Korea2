Headline findings

  • No tested strategy cleared all nine gates. The best of the 21 is S10_wind15_under (+3.8% holdout ROI, 6 of 9 gates) — it fails significance and calibration, so it is published as a lead, not a result.
  • Closing lines are hard to beat, and this model does not beat them. Its spread error is 10.647 points against 10.239 for the market close; every model-vs-market strategy loses money before and after vig.
  • Correlations are where the real value sits. Home cover and over are almost independent (phi = 0.0101), but moneyline and spread legs in the same game are not (phi = 0.6493) — mispricing those two together is a pricing problem, not a prediction problem.
  • Quarterback and receiver prop legs are strongly correlated in the one same-team pair with a clean log (Green Bay: r = 0.9029, n = 8); pricing those legs independently understates the joint probability by 71.72%.
  • Control strategies behave as expected: backing every home team returns -8.26% on the holdout, which is the vig floor and the benchmark any 'skill' claim has to clear.
