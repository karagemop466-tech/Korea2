INDEPENDENT NFL RESEARCH

Originate the line.
Then try to prove it wrong.

This workspace builds its own NFL spreads, totals, moneylines and player-prop lines from the official play-by-play feed, measures the correlations that decide parlay prices, and puts every candidate strategy through ten validation gates. The point is not to publish picks — it is to find out, honestly, which signals survive contact with the closing line.

Current verdict: nothing has earned capital.
The pipeline runs end to end on live 2026 data, and the answer this week is that the closing market beats our model. That is a result, not a failure.

What is actually verified

  • 7,548 NFL games (1999-2026) with closing lines, downloaded and hashed.
  • 208,231 player-weeks (2015-2026) for prop and correlation work.
  • Spread sign convention proven from the data, not assumed.
  • Every strategy test run out-of-sample from 2023 onward.

What is deliberately blank

  • No historical player-prop prices exist in the feed, so no prop ROI is published.
  • No verified execution access, limits or jurisdiction.
  • No funded prize pool for the leaderboard rewards.
  • No injury-report source confirmed, so injury signals are blocked.
