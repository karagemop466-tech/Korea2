INDEPENDENT NFL RESEARCH

Originate the line.
Then try to prove it wrong.

This workspace builds its own NFL spreads, totals, moneylines and player-prop lines from community-maintained nflverse game and weekly-stat extracts (not directly published by the NFL), measures the correlations that decide parlay prices, and puts every candidate strategy through ten validation gates. The point is not to publish picks — it is to find out, honestly, which signals survive contact with the closing line.

Research snapshot through 2026-11-01 — not current to the live slate.
Inputs are community-maintained nflverse extracts, not an NFL-published feed. The official NFL Week 10 schedule is newer than this snapshot; Week 9 / next-slate projections are stale. In this snapshot the closing market beats the model and no strategy is approved.

What is actually verified

  • 7,548 NFL games (1999-2026) with closing lines, downloaded and hashed.
  • 208,231 player-weeks (2015-2026) for prop and correlation work.
  • Spread sign convention proven from the data, not assumed.
  • Every strategy test run out-of-sample from 2023 onward.
Method & data →

What is deliberately blank

  • No historical player-prop prices exist in the feed, so no prop ROI is published.
  • No verified execution access, limits or jurisdiction.
  • No funded prize pool for the leaderboard rewards.
  • NFL.com publishes a live injury report; historical point-in-time snapshots and verified depth order are not ingested, so injury/depth adjustments remain blocked.
