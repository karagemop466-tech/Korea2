What the evidence supports today

DESIGN · NOT PERFORMANCE

Recommendation

Build a probability-first, event-timestamped forecasting system. Estimate distributions for outcomes, convert them into fair probabilities and only then compare against timestamped, legal-to-use prices. Keep independent singles as the primary test bed; treat parlays as a separate, dependence-aware product.

Use official NFL player, team and Next Gen Stats as traceable candidate inputs. Historical odds, exact quote timestamps, settlement rules, and a versioned play-by-play/outcome dataset are missing; therefore this project cannot responsibly publish an actionable NFL line or a leaderboard winner yet.

Executive decision: fund data acquisition and point-in-time backtest infrastructure before claiming an edge or staking real money.
