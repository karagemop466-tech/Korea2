Source register

Every citation, with what it actually says

Two tiers are used and they are never mixed. official means the publisher is the National Football League. third_party means a redistributor or aggregator whose numbers are not published by the league. A third-party row is marked 3P wherever it appears, so a reader can weigh it.

Every number published by this hub must resolve to one of these entries or to an explicit blank. 'official' means the publisher is the National Football League. 'third_party' means a redistributor/aggregator whose numbers are not published by the league; those rows are labelled as such on every page and are never described as official.
