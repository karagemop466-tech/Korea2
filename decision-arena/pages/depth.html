Injuries & depth

Who is out is easy. Who replaces him is the question.

Losing a first-string player to his backup and losing him to a fourth-string player are different football teams, and a report that counts only "starters out" cannot tell them apart. This page publishes the official report, the measured replacement depth, and the reason the second half cannot yet be completed here.

CONFIRMED_ABSENT: the league publishes no machine-readable depth chart. NFL.com returns its 404 page at the team depth-chart path; the official injury report carries no depth column. Consequence: No row on this page claims an OFFICIAL depth rank. The depth rank used is measured from participation, and labelled as such.
