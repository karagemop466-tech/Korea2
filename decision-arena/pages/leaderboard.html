Leaderboard

Signal ranks. Staking follows.

A strategy can win money on a bad signal and lose money on a good one. This board therefore publishes two separate columns and ranks on the first: the beat-the-close rate (was our side right against the closing number, regardless of price) and the ROI (what a fixed 1-unit stake would have banked). A lucky staking run cannot lift a strategy whose signal is negative, because signal sorts first.
