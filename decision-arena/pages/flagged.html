What a reader should conclude

  • No price in this repository can be called an opening or a closing number without a timestamp.
  • No player-prop ROI exists, because no prop price exists.
  • No official depth rank exists to cite; every depth number is measured, not published.
  • No strategy is live, and the reward pool is unfunded.
  • The results snapshot is stale by at least one completed week.

Those five statements are the reason this site is published as a research record rather than as a service. Each will be removed from this list when its source exists, not when it becomes inconvenient.

Research and measurement, not betting advice. Values are computed by the code in this repository from the sources on the source register; anything that could not be confirmed from a source is left blank and listed on Flagged for review.
