Correlation

Computed from box scores. Never from a price.

A parlay's price depends on how its legs move together. That number has to come from what players did, not from what a book thought they would do, so every coefficient here is computed from the per-player box scores alone. No market number, no line and no model output enters any value on this page. Each coefficient carries its n and a Fisher-z interval.

Correlation here is descriptive, not a test that selects a strategy. It is published with n and an interval so a reader can judge the precision; the multiple-testing correction is applied where selection happens, on the strategy page.
