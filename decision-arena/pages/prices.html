Price snapshots

What we actually know about each number

A price snapshot is one observation of one market on one game. Whether it can be called an opening or a closing number is a question about its provenance, not about the value. This page answers that question per snapshot and refuses to upgrade a grade without a timestamp.

The feed stores one price per market and no sampling time. So 7,103 of 7,103 snapshots are graded unattributed_single_observation. They are published because they are what exists, and labelled because calling them closing prices would be an invented claim.
