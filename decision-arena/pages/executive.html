3. Signal and staking are separate, and both are mostly empty

Ranking is by gates, then by beat-the-close rate, then by ROI. A lucky staking run cannot lift a strategy with a negative signal because signal sorts first. The best-ranked strategy is Model-edge moneyline with 6 of 10 gates and a beat-the-close rate of —.
