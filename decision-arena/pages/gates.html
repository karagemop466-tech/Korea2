Gates & demotion

How a strategy gets in, and how it is thrown out

Promotion is hard and demotion is automatic. Every rule on this page is numeric, is computed by the build, and fires without a judgement call. A live strategy that stops working returns to paper on the next evaluation; paper is the default state and live is the exception.
