Origination

Side first. Then the total. Then the moneyline.

The order is a dependency, not a preference: the total model consumes the side model, and the moneyline is derived from the side model's own margin distribution. A market whose prerequisite is missing is emitted as blocked_by_side rather than guessed. The fair number, the market's number and the difference each get their own column so a reader can disagree with the model without losing the market's view.
