Independent NFL signal research

Originate the line.
Then prove it, or say you cannot.

This workspace prices its own side, total and moneyline in that order, keeps the model's fair number, the market's number and the difference in separate columns, records what it can and cannot know about each price, and runs every candidate strategy through ten gates that separate signal from staking. Where an input is missing, the value is blank and the reason is published.

Nothing here is live and no value is invented. 12 of 12 strategies are REJECTED, 0 are PAPER and 0 are LIVE. Of 7,103 price snapshots, 7,103 cannot be called opening or closing because the feed states no sampling time.

What is verified

  • 7,088 settled regular-season games with scores, re-derived from the scores.
  • All 15 Week 9 2026 finals read from NFL.com and reconciled.
  • 28 clubs transcribed from the official Week 10 report; zero status disagreements against an independent transcription.
  • Every correlation published with n and a Fisher-z interval.
Verification log →

What is deliberately blank

  • No timestamped opening prices, so no per-bet closing-line value and no line-move signal.
  • No historical prop prices, so the prop families are EXECUTION_BLOCKED, not tested.
  • No official depth chart, so no row claims an official depth rank.
  • No funded prize pool, so the tiers rank and do not pay.
