Executive Summary

What this site is

A decision-support research site for NFL line origination and strategy validation. It originates its own NFL lines from official data, measures parlay/correlation relationships, runs strategies through validation gates, tracks injuries, and publishes a suggestions section and a pre-decision Q&A register so changes are reviewed before implementation.

Correlations remove the independence illusion

Same-game parlay legs are NOT independent. QB passing yards↔WR1 receiving yards r≈+0.56 to +0.67; pricing legs independently understates joint probability. Cross-game correlation ≈0. Prices for combos must be correlation-aware.

Luck is not removable; records do not persist

Pythagorean luck does not persist year-over-year (r≈0.019). A spread coin-flip test returned 50.0% (p=1.0). Do not extrapolate win-loss records as signal.
