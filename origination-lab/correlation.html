Measured phi coefficients with confidence intervals for every leg pair that appears in a parlay, plus the key-number distribution that governs pushes.

Method. Pearson phi between binary leg outcomes. Pushes are dropped because a push settles the leg at 0 profit and is not a Bernoulli trial. 95% CI from the Fisher z transform; p-value from n*phi^2 ~ chi-square(1). |phi| > 0.054 is significant at p<0.05 for n=3400.

Game-market leg pairs

The moneyline and the spread are the same event measured twice, so φ ≈ 0.67 is expected. What matters for parlay pricing is the pairs that are not mechanically linked: spread × total is essentially zero (φ = 0.025), which means a same-game spread+total ticket can be priced with the product without much error — but only because those legs happen to be independent, not because same-game legs are independent in general.

Key numbers

The distribution of NFL margins is heavily concentrated on 3, 4, 6, 7, 10 and 14. Any strategy that moves or avoids those numbers (teasers, buying points, alternate spreads) is trading in push probabilities, not just cover probabilities — which is why the push table is part of the correlation dataset rather than a footnote.
