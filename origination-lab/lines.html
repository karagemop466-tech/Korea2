How the line is built. Ridge-regularised team offence/defence ratings in a two-equation points model (home points and away points), fitted with exponential recency weights on every game played before the week being predicted. A rest differential is estimated as a free coefficient, and the home-field constant comes out of the fit. The spread is the predicted margin rounded to the nearest half point; the total is the sum of the two predicted scores; the moneyline is the normal CDF of the margin with σ = 13.6 points. Nothing from the game itself, or from later weeks, enters its own prediction.

Where the model earns its keep — and where it does not

Above about three points of disagreement the hit rate and the return both rise monotonically. That is the signature of a real signal rather than noise, but the sample at that threshold is far too small to clear the gates: 1,533 bets at 4+ points produces p = 0.172. The honest reading is "promising, unproven".
