Provenance. The datasets are published by nflverse and pulled from the publisher's git tree at a pinned commit, because the release-asset CDN is unreachable from the build shell. Two independent extracts of the closing line are reconciled against each other; points scored and allowed reconcile exactly with the standings file for all 800 team-seasons. Where the two closing line extracts disagree, the gap is published rather than smoothed.
