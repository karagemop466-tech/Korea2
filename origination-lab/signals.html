Twenty-seven pregame signals specified in this repository, scored with a false-discovery correction and an out-of-sample window that none of them survive.

Why the answer is 'none'. Twenty-seven tests at p < 0.05 would be expected to throw up about one and a third false positives by chance alone. Controlling the false discovery rate and then demanding the same sign on an untouched 2016-2026 window removes almost all of that. Nothing here clears both bars — and the two runners-up, low totals and cold-weather unders, actually flip sign out of sample, which is exactly what a fluke does.

What this does and does not prove

  • It does prove that this particular set of public, structural angles is not worth betting at the closing price on its own — the confidence intervals on every ROI straddle zero.
  • It does not prove that no signal exists. The scan is deliberately coarse (binary buckets, closing prices, no line shopping). A sharper version would need pregame injury news, practice reports and multiple books, none of which are in the verified extracts.
  • Selection is the enemy. The scan is one family of 27 tests reported in full; the strategies on the leaderboard are the second family, and gate G2 corrects across both.
