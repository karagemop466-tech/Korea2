SIGNAL CATALOGUE

Seven signal designs for originating lines - and the input each one waits for.

PROPOSALS. Nothing in this file is a measurement or a verified strategy. Every row states a test that has not been run, plus the input it is waiting for. Numbers that already exist in this repository are not repeated here - they are read from the committed registers by build_site.py and linked to their own file. A signal row is a *design*: hypothesis, required inputs, how it would be tested, and what stops it today. A row whose status is 'blocked' cannot be implemented no matter how it is coded, because the input does not exist in verified form.

Designs, not results

'Blocked' means no amount of coding fixes it today, because the input does not exist in verifiable form. 'Partially testable' means the accuracy half can run now and the profitability half cannot.

SIG-01
Starting-QB absence		single bet - spread and totalA confirmed absence of the designated starting quarterback moves a game's expected margin more than the posted line adjusts, in the first hours after the news breaks.Point-in-time 'who starts' record per team-game, kickoff timestamp, opening and closing spread/total, book and time of each price.blockedT03 (no archived point-in-time availability record) and T02 (no opening-line archive). Today's slate shows why a live read is not enough: the report lists Kyler Murray (QB) Out for MIN and Sam Darnold (QB) Out for SEA with Drew Lock starting - a fact published on Friday, not a fact stored anywhere…Event study: for each QB-absence announcement, measure the close-vs-open move and the residual margin against a model that already prices the close. One-sided test, Holm or Benjamini-Hochberg correction across all signal variants, minimum 300 qualifying games before any claim.If the close has already absorbed the move (residual slope ~ 0), the signal is worthless as a bet and only useful as a feature for later-prop pricing.NFL official Week 10 2026 injury report
SIG-02
Backup-quality delta (1st vs 2nd/3rd/4th string)		single bet - spread, team totalsThe cost of an absence depends on who replaces the absent player, so the same 'Out' tag has different value for a deep team and a thin one.A per-position depth ordering with a published source, plus the replacement's prior-season performance.blocked - hardT04. The league publishes no official depth chart at any path the sibling hubs could reach, and the club charts that do exist are labelled unofficial, are stale on at least five clubs, and omit the backup entirely at several starter-out positions (see each hub's own flags). Until a string source is…Build a replacement-value index from snaps and participation rather than from any chart, then test the index against closing-line residuals. Publish the index definition next to every result.If the index adds nothing beyond a plain 'count of Out players', drop it - one number is better than two.NFL official Week 10 2026 injury report
SIG-03
Defensive deficiency: shared absences in a secondary or front seven		single bet - team totals, alt totalsTwo or more Out designations in the same defensive unit (e.g. both starting cornerbacks) degrade points allowed more than the market's linear adjustment, because the third-string player's snap share jumps.Position-group absence counts from the official report (available), snap share by defender for the replacement (available in a third-party feed), and a price for the alt total (not available).partially testableT02 for the price, but the *accuracy* half can be tested now: predict team points allowed from unit-absence counts and compare with the realised total. The root pipeline already measures the market's total at MAE 10.617 on the same games, which is the benchmark to beat.Walk-forward regression on unit-absence counts by position group, target = team points; then test whether the residual correlates with the closing total. Sample floor from the repo's own power table before any significance claim.If unit-absence counts add nothing over team-level counts, the 'which unit' detail is noise and should not be published.Line Lab committed pipeline results (data/derived)
SIG-04
Offensive-line depletion vs sack and pressure props		player prop comboMissing starters on the offensive line raise pass-rush product prices (sacks, pressures, QB hits) for the opposing edge rusher more than the passing-yards line moves.OL absence count per team-game (from the official report), a prop price series (absent), snap counts for the replacement (third-party feed).blockedT02. Prop ROI cannot be measured at all in this repository because no historical player-prop prices exist in the feed the pipeline consumes; the root site states this rather than inventing a price.Calibrate a sack-count model first (accuracy only), then once prices exist, evaluate as CLV, not ROI.If the accuracy gain over a naive rate is inside the interval, stop.Line Lab committed pipeline results (data/derived)
SIG-05
Same-game parlay legs priced with measured dependence		parlay / prop comboFor same-team legs (QB passing yards + WR1 receiving yards), the market's multiplied-and-discounted parlay price mis-states the joint probability; a copula or direct joint estimate finds the difference.A parlay price series, or a house rule for same-game legs; plus the joint outcome distribution (available in the committed extracts).partially testableT02/T06 for the price. The dependence side is measurable today, and the hubs already disagree on its size - see the Reconciliation page: the same label carries different coefficients across hubs because 'WR1' is defined differently (designated starter, projected starter, actual game leader).Freeze one definition per leg, publish each coefficient with its interval and n, then compare the joint exceedance rate to the independence product on out-of-sample seasons.If a single canonical definition collapses the spread between published coefficients to ~0, the disagreement was definitional and the parlay edge may be too small to trade.Line Lab committed pipeline results (data/derived)
SIG-06
Availability-delta line move (news-to-close latency)		single bet - spread, any marketThe bet is on the gap between the Friday/saturday availability news and the close, not on the news itself; a strategy that only reads the close is measuring the market's own skill.Timestamped line history (absent), timestamped availability capture (buildable).blockedT02. This is the reason the CLV-logging proposal ranks where it does: without timestamps there is no way to distinguish a skill claim from a race to the close.Log every price with its source and time; measure median absolute price improvement vs close after the fact; report as CLV, with a minimum-holdout rule so the leaderboard cannot be gamed by a single lucky week.If price improvement is indistinguishable from zero, the strategy has no edge to develop.NFL official Week 10 2026 injury report
SIG-07
Inactives-minus-report gap		feature engineering, all marketsThe gap between 'players listed Out on the report' and 'players actually declared inactive 90 minutes before kickoff' is a measurable, usable signal - and a data-quality trap for anyone building features from the report alone.Per-game inactive lists (public on the official site as game news), the report for the same game (available), and a price series (absent).testable now for data quality, blocked for pricingnothing for the first half. Today's capture already demonstrates the trap: four clubs printed 'No Injuries Reported' in one hub's earlier capture while the same day's official news listed injuries for them, and the Monday-night game carries no game-status designations at all in the Friday report.Build the inactive-vs-report difference table for every game in one week, measure how often a player declared Out plays and how often an unlisted player is inactive. Publish the error rate as a feature-quality note on every availability-based page.If the report and the inactives agree almost always, drop the reconciliation step and document the single exception class (rested players).NFL news roundup - Friday, Nov 13, 2026
