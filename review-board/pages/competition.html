LEADERBOARD & REWARDS

A competition where rank is earned by clearing gates, not by hitting a return.

A strategy = a written rule + a market + a stake rule + a settlement rule, submitted as code with a fixed seed. No rule text, no entry. Whether any prize is ever funded is cluster T09 and an owner decision; nothing in this repository records a funded pool, and no hub may state a payout as available.

Removing luck

Anti-luck controls

Each control targets a failure already documented in this repository: pooled-ATS arithmetic, variant fishing, single-season spikes, repriced parlay legs.
#ControlSource
1A final holdout window is sealed: no entry may be re-run against it more than once, and the count of runs against it is published.program.json
2A negative control (all home teams, all overs) enters every batch and is published next to the candidates; a candidate that does not beat its own control is not ranked.program.json
3Every batch declares how many variants were tried; the multiple-comparison correction uses that number, not the number that survived.program.json
4Pushes and voids are settled by the written operator rule and removed from the win-rate denominator; the removal count is shown.program.json
5The leaderboard ranks on intervals: an entry whose confidence interval overlaps break-even is marked 'not distinguishable from zero' regardless of its point estimate.program.json
