Where the absences bite - and where the evidence stops.

The brief asks the system to know when a second, third or fourth-stringer is replacing a starter. The official report says who is out; nothing official says who comes in. The two right-hand columns are therefore blank on every row, and the gap is a decision (clusters T03 and T04), not a coding task.
