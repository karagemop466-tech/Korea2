DECISION-READINESS REGISTER

114 questions, normalised into 12 readiness clusters.

Each hub's own decision-readiness register is read as-is and normalised to three states: answered (the hub records an answer and no gap), partially answered (an answer and a stated gap), unanswered (a stated gap and no answer). A cluster is only 'closed' when no hub has an open item in it; disagreement between hubs is shown side by side, never averaged. State totals: answered 38, partially answered 67, unanswered 9.

'Answered' here means a hub recorded an answer against a source it read.
It does not mean the answer is externally verifiable today. Where a claim depends on data this repository does not hold - prop prices, depth strings, a jurisdiction determination - the row stays open on this board even when a hub marks it answered in principle.
