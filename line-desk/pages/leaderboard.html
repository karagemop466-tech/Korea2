Signal vs staking

AS OF 2026-11-14

Signal and staking are measured and ranked separately. The signal rank uses only gate outcomes and outcome statistics (gates, BH-adjusted p, raw p, sample). Staking metrics (ROI and its interval) are reported in their own block and are NEVER an input to the signal rank, so a lucky staking run cannot mask a bad signal. Ranking staking separately is for transparency only; it earns nothing.

Signal rank rule: gates passed desc, then BH-adjusted p asc, then raw p asc, then decided n desc. Staking rank rule: ROI desc (reported only, never used for rewards or promotion). The committed leaderboard ranks 'by gates passed, then by ROI' (data/derived/leaderboard.json). This desk separates the two blocks instead; PS-F3.
