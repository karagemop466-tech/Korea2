Gates & demotion

AS OF 2026-11-14

PROPOSED design - thresholds are proposals from this project, not an official standard; the CLV-side gate and its tests live in price-store/scripts/gates.py (parallel session).

A strategy is PAPER until it passes promotion checks, then LIVE, and can be sent back to PAPER by a demotion gate. Staking metrics never promote a strategy; a lucky ROI run alone cannot keep a bad signal live.

States: PAPER → LIVE — promotion only through P1-P3; demotion only through D1/D2/D3.
