Honest state of play

The verified results feed and the official-only correlation set are live and sourced. The price-snapshot store exists as a contract with 0 market captures because no official price source exists (the NFL's official betting feed goes to operators via an exclusive distributor). Because prices are absent, closing-line value cannot be measured, no strategy can be promoted to live, and the demotion gate has no subject — it is defined, dry-run and waiting. 7 of 7 candidates stay PAPER. Every blocked value is blank and listed on Flagged for review.

Line Desk — research and measurement, not betting advice. Values that cannot be confirmed from an official source are left blank and flagged.
