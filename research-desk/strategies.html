02 / PRICING LOGIC

From forecasts to fair prices

01

Define the contract

Specify market, threshold, overtime, push, void and payout terms. Treat participation and settlement as part of the event definition.

02

Price the outcome

For a binary no-push contract, fair decimal odds = 1 / p. Expected net return per unit at decimal odds d = p × d − 1. For push/void outcomes, model all settlement states explicitly.

03

Compare responsibly

Compare against an executable timestamped quote and its rules, including margin. For a two-sided no-push market, proportional de-vig uses implied q / (q₁ + q₂); it is an approximation, not an observed true probability.

The pricing arithmetic is a proposed analytical convention; the regulator explains why rules, payouts and likelihood matter. Read regulator guidance ↗

!
Never interpret a missing price as zero.

The system must abstain when price, time, rules, availability or reliable inputs are missing. This site does not place bets.
