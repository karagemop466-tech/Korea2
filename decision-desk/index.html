Standing rules on this site

  • Values that cannot be confirmed from an official source are left blank and flagged — never inferred.
  • Every data row's last cell links to its source (fetch date 2026-11-12).
  • Team depth charts are team-published and mostly labelled Unofficial by the teams themselves.
  • Flagged items do not block the merge; they live on the Flagged for Review page.
  • Suggestions are proposals only — the Suggestions register waits for a reviewer's decision.
